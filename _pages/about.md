salapakalab develops open-source AI methods to understand and engineer force-bearing proteins.

Our ForceFold program focuses on:

- extracting clean single-molecule force spectroscopy signals from noisy data,
- resolving domain-level mechanics in heterogeneous proteins,
- and designing targeted mechanotherapies through inverse modeling.

We are building a public ecosystem of datasets, tools, and benchmarks for mechanobiology research.

👉 Visit the [ForceFold project page](/projects/#work) for details.
