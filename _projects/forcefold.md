## Overview

**ForceFold** is an open-source AI platform from **salapakalab** to accelerate single-molecule force spectroscopy analysis and enable inverse design of force-targeted proteins.

## Why this matters

Protein mechanical failures contribute to severe diseases including Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD), cardiomyopathies, and cancer metastasis. Current analysis and simulation pipelines are too slow for high-throughput therapeutic discovery.

## Platform components

- **PemNN**: physics-augmented filtering for isolating single-molecule events from noisy multi-molecule traces.
- **LatentUnfold**: physics-aware deep clustering to deconvolve heterogeneous multi-domain proteins.
- **GenUnfold**: physics-guided diffusion model for forward prediction and inverse design of mechanical trajectories.
- **ForceFold loop**: integrates AI models with AlphaFold/ProteinMPNN and experimental validation.

## Impact goals

- Reduce data analysis time from days to under an hour.
- Expand open mechanobiology datasets for the research community.
- Support high-throughput screening of therapeutic protein candidates.
- Enable modular inverse design of multi-domain mechanoproteins.

## Planned milestones

1. Automated filtering and yield optimization for single-molecule data.
2. Domain-level deconvolution for heterogeneous proteins.
3. Generative prediction of unfolding trajectories.
4. End-to-end inverse modular design and experimental validation.

## Outreach

We will share datasets, code, and model updates as open-source resources to support global academic and biopharma collaboration.
